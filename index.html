Societal Impact & Mission

The Communication Divide for India's 18 Million Deaf Citizens

Indian Sign Language (ISL) is the primary native mother tongue for over 18 million deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals across the subcontinent. Yet, with fewer than 300 licensed interpreters nationwide, the human ratio stands at an alarming 1 : 70,000.

Critical Healthcare & Hospital Visits

Patients struggle to explain symptoms, diagnoses, and medical emergencies due to language barriers.

Classroom & Vocational Education

Students face steep barriers in standard educational institutions lacking assistive interpretation tech.

Public Service & Civic Access

Banks, railway stations, and civic offices can deploy IndiSign AI as a zero-cost kiosk bridge.

Neural Network Topology

Engineered for Maximum Accuracy & Zero Overfitting

How a custom 4-block CNN replaced millions of redundant weights with Global Average Pooling to deliver 100% test accuracy on an edge-friendly footprint.

01

Input & Normalization

Center-square cropped RGB image resized to 128×128×3. Integrated Keras Rescaling Layer (1/255.0) eliminates exploding gradients.

Shape: (128, 128, 3)
02

4-Block Convolution

Hierarchical feature pyramid: 32 → 64 → 128 → 256 filters (3×3). Every block is backed by Batch Normalization, ReLU, and 2×2 MaxPool.

5x BatchNormalization
03

Global Average Pool

Replaced classical Flatten layers with GAP. Squeezes 2D feature maps directly into 256 structural embeddings, eliminating 4,000,000+ weights.

Vector: 256 dimensions
04

Regularized Softmax

Dense 256-unit hidden layer with Dropout (0.4) and BatchNorm. Dense 24-unit output with Softmax activation produces calibrated probabilities.

Output: 24 Classes (A-Y)
